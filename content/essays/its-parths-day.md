### A Quick Story on Kindness:

Some days this year felt like the end of the world. Yet, looking back, there's so much to be proud of. One incident sticks with me:

I was biking to my favourite chai spot when I saw a 65-year-old uncle pushing his Activa toward a petrol pump, half a kilometre away. I stopped and offered help, and his eyes teared up. maybe he'd started to doubt that kindness still exists. Ten minutes later, tank filled, he smiled wide. I giggled the whole way back.

This isn't bragging. If you get a chance to help, just do it. It comes back often when you need it most.

### Social Media Is (Mostly) Doomed:

Hot take: most of us can't recall the last ten reels we watched. Our brains weren't built for constant noise. Wars, disasters. if you can help, help. if not, drowning in headlines only drains you.

Validation is another trap. 20 likes on a story out of 300 views feels nice, but it fades fast. Everyone has their own thousand problems. No one's truly watching.

I'm not quitting the apps completely, and you don't have to either - just remember the only resource you truly own is time. Spend it wisely.
