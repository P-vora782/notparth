## the setup

i run @designgyanbyrahul with rahul. the goal was never the account it is distribution first, then we can build whatever we want on top of it.

problem: rahul runs dacoit, his agency. he has zero time but he loves to experiment.

so instead of asking him for hours he doesn't have, we built something that does the hours.

we use an agent called hermes. we named ours reelpaglu.

## the 7 steps

1. **sourcing.** every morning at 8, reelpaglu goes through twitter and finds what actually worked yesterday in design, ai and tech. it drops a list of 10 ideas in our group. i wake up, look for the one that makes me think more people should know this, and that sounds like rahul. i pick one.
2. **script.** reelpaglu goes back and learns more about that idea, then writes a script built around rahul's persona. keywords that work, shock value early, a few other parameters we set in a skill. if the script is weak i tell it what is off. we go back and forth. then it's locked.
3. **audio.** the script goes to elevenlabs where we have an account with rahul's voice. a sub-judge agent listens and decides if it actually sounds like him or needs a redo.
4. **video.** once audio passes, it goes to heygen where we have rahul's avatar. same sub-judge does qa on the output.
5. **layout.** with audio and video ready, reelpaglu pulls relevant b-rolls and builds a layout sheet, basically a shot by shot map of what the reel is going to look like.
6. **final output.** i approve the layout sheet, the edit comes back in minutes.
7. **handoff.** caption written with the right keywords, download link in my hands. done.

## cost

→ the agent itself costs us almost nothing. rahul already pays for codex for his own use and the agent runs on the same.

→ sourcing ideas, around $20/month in api cost.

→ elevenlabs, roughly $1 per minute of audio.

→ heygen, roughly $1 per minute of video.

→ claude api for scripts, around $5/month.

comes to about $80 to $90 a month.

and you can strip the agent out entirely and do these steps yourself. that puts you under a third of this. cost is not the reason you aren't posting.

## does it actually work?

→ 700k+ impressions and 3,000+ followers on the account.

→ rahul built matwidget.com. one reel sent 300 users to it in 24 hours, and then two creators with 600k combined reach picked it up and shared it on their own.

→ inbound and real connections with people he had never met before.
