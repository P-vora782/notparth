too late to post this, but 2025 was a hell of a ride.

I started last year by getting an apartment and living in a different city, Bangalore.

Parth, who had just joined a startup and the crypto industry, knew he had to live in Bangalore.

December'24, I crashed at a friend's place and then found my little, too cute apartment.

![](/essays/2025-year-in-review/1.jpg)

It was the first time moving away from home for a long undefined time

Somehow, I survived.

2025 has changed a lot in me. And at least now I know it's a long road before I reach where I want to be.

Here's my small proof of movement:

- 2023 - 1st flight
- 2024 - 11 flights
- 2025 - 18 flights + 1 train

All within India. Because I promised myself I'd explore my own country first.

Ooty, Coonoor, Munnar, Delhi, Rishikesh, Haridwar, Mussoorie, Jaipur, Mysore, Bangalore, Nagpur, Pune, Goa, and a few more.

![](/essays/2025-year-in-review/2.jpg)

Along the way, I:

- started a memecoin trading fund (raised $26k)
- bought a MacBook
- bought AirPods, lost them, bought again, broke them again (😭)
- started creating video content (50k+ impressions)
- got my first credit card
- bungee jumping, river rafting
- spent my birthday sitting in a temple
- got a wisdom tooth (removed)
- attended concerts of my favs Prateek Kuhad & Lucky Ali (kinda cool)
- watched sunset in the mountain and on a beach
- went all in every time (poker energy)
- lived a little

and right before hosting my first event, got into an accident and walked away with 5 stitches :D

This year gave me a lot. It also shook me from within.

I'll always have something to miss, and something to look forward to. But I'm grateful I moved - physically, mentally, emotionally.

**What's next?**

Honestly, I don't know. And I'm not trying to figure it all out at once.

You'll see me show up more.
Do random things.
Execute.
